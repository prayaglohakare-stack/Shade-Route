# Field survey data

Use the collection and validation procedure in [VIT field survey specification](../../docs/VIT_FIELD_SURVEY_SPEC.md). Keep raw OSM data under `data/osm/` unchanged. Store survey source evidence and reviewed corrections here with stable IDs, dates, non-identifying team codes, methods, GPS accuracy metadata, access evidence and verification status.

Do not record personal names, faces, student IDs or personal location histories. Do not route across restricted, unclear or unknown-access paths. Do not add shade values without separate evidence. Retrospective qualitative visit notes are recorded in the survey specification; no GPS-verified paths, junctions, or route edges have been accepted yet.
