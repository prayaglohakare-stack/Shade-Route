GPS accuracy decision aid

≤ 5 m: preferred for a stationary junction/endpoint if it agrees with the feature and repeat observation. > 5 to 10 m: conditional; require landmark and repeat/second-device support. > 10 m, missing or unstable: do not use alone for a narrow path, endpoint, or junction; recollect. These are project acceptance bands, not a phone-GPS guarantee.
